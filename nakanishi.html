Project overview

Technical clarity for specialized dental instruments

This assignment involved translating 14,699 words of technical documentation for dental and oral surgery instruments from US English into Canadian French. The work required consistent terminology across product names, components, accessories, safety warnings and user instructions.

The central challenge was to preserve specialized technical meaning while producing clear, natural French for professional users in the Canadian dental market. SDL Trados Studio supported the translation workflow and terminology consistency.

At a glance

  • Client/context: Nakanishi
  • Language direction: US English → Canadian French
  • Documented volume: 14,699 words
  • Domain: Dental and oral surgery instruments
  • Workflow tool: SDL Trados Studio

Linguistic focus

Consistency where details matter

  • Terminology: Maintain stable names for instruments, components and accessories throughout the documentation.
  • Safety information: Preserve the meaning and clarity of warnings and instructions for use.
  • Target audience: Balance technical precision with natural Canadian French suited to dental professionals.

Key takeaway

In specialized medical-technology documentation, terminology must reflect how components relate to one another, while warnings and instructions remain clear and consistent. Technical accuracy and readable Canadian French work together to help professional users interpret the documentation as intended.
