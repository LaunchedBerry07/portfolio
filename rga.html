Project overview

Precision across related reinsurance mandates

This assignment covered 5,426 words across two related mandates in the specialized field of reinsurance. The work required careful handling of financial, risk-management and contractual terminology in Canadian French. Because the assignments were delivered across separate documentary and billing batches, terminological consistency was an important part of maintaining accuracy across the work.

At a glance

  • Client / context: RGA Reinsurance Group / Company
  • Language direction: English → Canadian French
  • Documented volume: 5,426 words across two mandates
  • Domain: Reinsurance; financial, risk-management and contractual terminology

Linguistic focus

What the work required

  • Specialized terminology — Use precise Canadian French terminology for financial, risk-management and contractual concepts.
  • Consistency across mandates — Maintain terminological stability across related assignments and separate delivery batches.
  • Contextual accuracy — Preserve the meaning of specialized content without flattening distinctions between related financial or contractual concepts.

Key takeaway

In specialized financial translation, consistency is part of accuracy. Related mandates may be delivered separately, but terminology must remain stable and the meaning of financial, risk-management and contractual content must be preserved throughout.
