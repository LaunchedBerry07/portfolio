Project checklist

Language services

For translation, localization, comparative revision, editing, proofreading, machine translation post-editing (MTPE), linguistic quality assurance (LQA) or terminology support, it helps to include:

  • The type of content and subject matter
  • The source and target languages, including the intended French-language market
  • The approximate word count, volume or overall scope
  • The intended use and format, such as a website, software interface, technical document or publication
  • Your target deadline and any specific quality, terminology or review requirements
  • Relevant reference files, glossaries, style guides or source materials, when available

You don’t need to have every detail settled before getting in touch. A brief description is enough to start the conversation; we can clarify the scope and requirements from there.

Language, AI or workflow projects

For a question involving language technology, AI-assisted content or workflow automation, describe the task or problem, the tools or systems involved, what you would like to improve, and any important constraints. If the idea is still taking shape, a short outline of the current situation is a useful starting point.
