Maintaining a consistent voice across a full-length book

This assignment involved translating Could It Be This Simple? A Biblical Model for Healing the Mind into Canadian French. The documented scope was 62,196 words, and the book was intended for church members.

The text addressed sensitive themes, including mental health, spirituality, forgiveness and interpersonal relationships. The translation called for sustained attention to voice and tone, with language that remained empathetic and pastorally appropriate throughout the book.

Editorial project

What the work required

Navigating sensitive subject matter while ensuring linguistic continuity across a vast volume of text.

Narrative voice

Maintain a coherent voice across an extensive text rather than treating each chapter as an isolated unit.

Tone and sensitivity

Handle discussions of mental health, spirituality, forgiveness and relationships with an empathetic, pastorally appropriate tone.

Terminological stability

Keep key wording and concepts consistent from the beginning to the end of the book.

Continuity is key
Every word matters

Long-form editorial translation depends on sustained decisions about voice, tone and terminology.

The challenge is to preserve continuity across the whole work while remaining attentive to the sensitivity of its subject matter.
