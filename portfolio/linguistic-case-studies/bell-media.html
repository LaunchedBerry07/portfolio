Making operational instructions clear and usable

This assignment involved full post-editing of 16,293 words of operational workflows and Visio “Blueprints” designed to guide call-centre agents.

The content needed to remain readable and actionable while following predefined glossaries, character limits and interface constraints. Quality checks focused on terminology, source–target alignment and the completeness of the translated content.

Operational Blueprint

What the work required

Ensuring that translated instructions are not just accurate, but strictly adhere to operational constraints and established guidelines.

Terminology control

Apply required glossary choices and strictly check for blacklisted terms across all materials.

Interface constraints

Respect character limits, spacing and the highly specific practical needs of operational screens and workflow charts.

Comparative QA

Check source–target alignment comprehensively and confirm that all translated content is fully complete and functional.

Beyond the words
Clarity = Efficiency

In operational workflows, linguistic quality is inseparable from usability.

MTPE requires more than correcting wording: the translated instructions must follow terminology requirements, fit interface constraints and remain clear enough for agents to use in context.
