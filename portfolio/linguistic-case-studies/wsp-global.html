Clear, consistent professional communication

This assignment involved linguistic revision and proofreading of corporate and professional documents for WSP Global, an organization working across engineering, science and infrastructure.

The review focused on correcting linguistic issues, checking wording in context and applying Canadian French conventions appropriate to professional business content. The aim was to deliver clear, coherent and polished text while preserving the intended meaning.

Corporate Style

Three priorities in the review

Elevating professional corporate communications through systematic linguistic precision.

Accuracy

Identify and correct linguistic issues while preserving the source content’s intended meaning.

Contextual coherence

Check that wording remains clear and appropriate within the surrounding professional material.

Professional style

Apply Canadian French conventions and maintain a polished register suited to an engineering-related business context.

Professional Voice
Polished text

Effective proofreading goes beyond correcting isolated errors.

It helps preserve meaning, strengthen clarity and maintain a consistent professional voice in the final text.
