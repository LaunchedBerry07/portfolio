About the Client

A global life and health reinsurer

Reinsurance Group of America, Incorporated (RGA) is a global life and health reinsurer headquartered in Chesterfield, Missouri. The company provides reinsurance solutions, financial expertise and risk-management services to insurance companies worldwide. With specialized knowledge in mortality, longevity and health-related risks, RGA helps insurers manage financial exposure and develop sustainable business strategies.

Project overview

Specialized financial translation in reinsurance

I completed two specialized financial translation mandates in the reinsurance sector, covering a combined total of 5,426 words translated from English into Canadian French.

These assignments involved financial, risk-management and contractual content within a highly specialized professional environment. They required a careful approach to linguistic accuracy, contextual interpretation and the precise use of industry-specific terminology.

Working with financial documentation calls for more than straightforward linguistic equivalence. Complex concepts must be conveyed faithfully, with particular attention to their intended meaning, professional context and appropriate terminology in the target language.

Translation focus

Working with specialized financial content

Reinsurance is a specialized field within the broader financial and insurance industries, with its own terminology and conceptual distinctions.

Throughout these assignments, I focused on accurately conveying financial information while preserving the meaning and nuances of the English source content. Particular attention was given to terminology associated with financial operations, risk management, reinsurance and contractual matters.

My approach emphasized clarity, precision and consistency, ensuring that specialized concepts remained understandable and appropriately expressed in Canadian French without losing their technical or professional meaning.

Scope of work

5,426 words across two mandates

Across two separate mandates, I translated a total of 5,426 words from English into Canadian French.

The assignments covered specialized content relating to:

  • Reinsurance: terminology and concepts specific to the reinsurance sector.
  • Financial operations: specialized financial language requiring precise and context-sensitive translation.
  • Risk management: terminology associated with financial risks and their management.
  • Contractual content: language requiring particular care to preserve meaning, nuance and accuracy.

Terminology & consistency

Accuracy across separate mandates

The two mandates were handled across multiple documentary and billing batches, making terminological consistency an important consideration throughout the assignments.

I paid particular attention to the accurate and consistent use of specialized financial terminology, taking into account the meaning of individual terms and their relationship to the broader context.

In financial and contractual content, seemingly minor differences in wording can affect how a concept is understood. Maintaining appropriate terminology and preserving the intended meaning are therefore essential to producing reliable professional translations.

My work focused on balancing terminological precision with natural, readable Canadian French, ensuring that the translated content remained faithful to the source while meeting the linguistic expectations of its professional context.
