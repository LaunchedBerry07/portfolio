About the Client

An Italian engineering and water-treatment specialist

R.E.M. srl International Mechanics is an Italian engineering and manufacturing company specializing in equipment for wastewater, sludge and drinking water treatment. Founded in 2003, the company designs solutions for municipal and industrial applications, including screening, conveying, sludge conditioning and dewatering systems. Its products are supplied to customers and industrial projects worldwide.

Project overview

Working across five industrial product families

I provided technical translation and comparative editing services for R.E.M. International Mechanics, working on user manuals for five industrial product families.

The assignment brought together two distinct workflows: translating English technical documentation into French and reviewing existing French translations against their English source texts. Across both services, I handled a total of 51,187 words.

Industrial systems

The challenge

Maintaining clarity across specialized equipment

The manuals covered equipment used in screw conveying, sludge management, water treatment and polymer preparation. Working across related equipment families required careful attention to specialized terminology, product nomenclature and functional distinctions. I focused on maintaining consistency while ensuring that each product's specific components, functions and applications were accurately conveyed.

Key considerations included:

  • Maintaining stable terminology across related equipment families.
  • Preserving distinctions between products, components and applications.
  • Conveying specialized industrial content accurately and clearly.

Scope of work

Two complementary language services

I translated five user manuals from English into French, then carried out a separate comparative editing assignment, reviewing French translations against their English source texts to identify discrepancies and improve accuracy.

The work covered:

  • Technical translation: 25,693 words across five product user manuals.
  • Comparative editing: 25,494 words reviewed against the English source texts.
  • Product coverage: STC, STCV, STCD, STPL and STPLL.

Translation workflow

Full translation, including repeated content

The source files contained repeated passages and exact segment matches in different locations. These internal repetitions were identified during the translation process and were included in the work performed.

I used SDL Trados Studio and created a translation memory for the assignment. No pre-existing client translation memory or terminology database was supplied. The repetitions and 100% matches in the analysis referred to content repeated within the source documents, not to translations retrieved from an existing client memory.

I translated the full 25,693-word volume.
