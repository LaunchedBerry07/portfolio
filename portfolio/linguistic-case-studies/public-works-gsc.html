Consistent terminology across institutional mandates

This body of work comprised 14 institutional and public-sector mandates totalling 101,450 words. The group included a 43,521-word core project. The translation work required attention to official government terminology, established stylistic conventions and an appropriate administrative register across separate deliveries.

TERMIUM Plus, the Government of Canada's terminology and linguistic data bank, was consulted to research and verify specialized terms. This reference-based approach supported terminological consistency and alignment with institutional usage.
