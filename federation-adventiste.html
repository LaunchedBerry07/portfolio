Project overview

Maintaining a consistent voice across a full-length book

This assignment involved translating *Could It Be This Simple? A Biblical Model for Healing the Mind* into Canadian French. The documented scope was 62,196 words, and the book was intended for church members.

The text addressed sensitive themes, including mental health, spirituality, forgiveness and interpersonal relationships. The translation called for sustained attention to voice and tone, with language that remained empathetic and pastorally appropriate throughout the book.

At a glance

  • Client / context: Fédération adventiste du Québec
  • Language direction: English → Canadian French
  • Documented volume: 62,196 words
  • Source work: Could It Be This Simple? A Biblical Model for Healing the Mind
  • Audience context: Book intended for church members

Editorial focus

What the work required

  • Narrative voice — Maintain a coherent voice across an extensive text rather than treating each chapter as an isolated unit.
  • Tone and sensitivity — Handle discussions of mental health, spirituality, forgiveness and relationships with an empathetic, pastorally appropriate tone.
  • Terminological stability — Keep key wording and concepts consistent from the beginning to the end of the book.

Key takeaway

Long-form editorial translation depends on sustained decisions about voice, tone and terminology. The challenge is to preserve continuity across the whole work while remaining attentive to the sensitivity of its subject matter.
