METHOD & QUALITY

A structured approach
to linguistic quality.

Good work starts before the first edit.

A reliable result depends on more than the work performed on the text itself. Preparation, reference management, verification and delivery all contribute to the quality of the final content.

The exact process varies by assignment. I tailor the level of intervention and verification to the content, scope and quality requirements rather than applying the same workflow to every project.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Clarify the purpose, audience, content type, language requirements and intended use.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Review project instructions, reference materials, terminology, style guides and relevant technical constraints.

  3. 03

    Translate, adapt
    or review

    Perform the linguistic work with attention to meaning, consistency, tone and the requirements of the assignment.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Apply appropriate checks for accuracy, terminology, formatting and compliance with agreed specifications.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Return the completed work in the agreed format, ready for its intended next step.

Rates &
terms.

The rates on this page are in Canadian dollars (CAD). Final pricing depends on volume, content type, resources, context and timeline.
