Project overview

Reviewing language within established brand rules

This assignment involved targeted linguistic revision and contextual proofreading for U.S. Bank, with emphasis on accuracy in context and compliance with the client’s established brand requirements.

Dedicated translation memories and project glossaries were used to align the reviewed content with approved formulations. One explicit brand rule was to preserve the name “U.S. Bank” in English rather than translate it.

At a glance

  • Client: U.S. Bank
  • Language direction: US English → Canadian French
  • Documented volume: 1,500 words
  • Assignment: Contextual linguistic revision and proofreading
  • Reference resources: Dedicated translation memory and project glossary

Linguistic focus

What the review prioritized

  • Contextual accuracy: Assess wording in context rather than treating each sentence as an isolated unit.
  • Resource alignment: Follow established formulations recorded in the translation memory and project glossary.
  • Brand consistency: Respect brand rules, including retaining “U.S. Bank” in its original form.

Key takeaway

A relatively compact assignment can still require disciplined quality control. Brand compliance and consistency with approved language resources are integral to a reliable final text.
