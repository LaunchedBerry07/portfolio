Project overview

Maintaining consistency across batches and content types

This assignment covered 30,638 words of Polaris Industries website content delivered across multiple batches. The work combined translation, full machine-translation post-editing and proofreading.

A dedicated three-part sequence for the Snow product line accounted for 12,359 words. Across the broader assignment, the priority was to maintain continuity between batches and service types so that product names, categories and technical specifications remained consistent throughout the website content.

At a glance

  • Client/context: Polaris Industries
  • Language direction: US English → Canadian French
  • Documented volume: 30,638 words
  • Featured sequence: Snow product line · 12,359 words across three parts
  • Content: Website content
  • Services: Translation, full MTPE and proofreading

Linguistic focus

What the work required

  • Terminology continuity: Keep product names, categories and technical specifications consistent across related pages.
  • Multi-batch coordination: Maintain editorial and terminology continuity across separate deliveries and service types.
  • Layered language work: Coordinate translation, full post-editing and proofreading for website content intended for a Canadian French audience.

Key takeaway

Web localization at scale depends on more than translating individual pages. Terminology and editorial choices need to remain stable across product lines, batches and different stages of linguistic processing.
