Project overview

Preserving distinctions across connected systems

The assignment involved translating the PM151 Operations and Maintenance Manual for the TSC 900 automatic transfer switch controller.

The documented material covers hardware components, operator interfaces, communication connections and automatic backup-generator controls. The translation needed to convey the technical information precisely and keep operating instructions clear and unambiguous for the people using the equipment.

At a glance

  • Client: Thomson Technology
  • Document: PM151 Operations and Maintenance Manual
  • Equipment: TSC 900 automatic transfer switch controller
  • Language direction: Canadian English → Canadian French
  • Documented volume: 21,931 words

Technical focus

Precision across connected systems and procedures

  • System distinctions: Keep the functions of the Graphical HMI Controller (GHC) distinct from those of the Switch Control Unit (SCU).
  • Operational clarity: Render electrical switching sequences, telemetry instructions and power-transition procedures in clear, unambiguous Canadian French.
  • Terminological consistency: Use stable terminology for hardware components, interfaces, connections and controller functions throughout the manual.

Key takeaway

Technical documentation must do more than convey information: it must distinguish system components and describe operating procedures clearly enough to support accurate use.
