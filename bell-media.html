Project overview

Making operational instructions clear and usable

This assignment involved full post-editing of 16,293 words of operational workflows and Visio “Blueprints” designed to guide call-centre agents. The content needed to remain readable and actionable while following predefined glossaries, character limits and interface constraints. Quality checks focused on terminology, source–target alignment and the completeness of the translated content.

At a glance

  • Client: Bell Media
  • Language direction: English → Canadian French
  • Documented volume: 16,293 words
  • Content: Call-centre workflows and Visio “Blueprints”
  • QA tool: Wordfast Transcheck

Linguistic focus

What the work required

  • Terminology control — Apply required glossary choices and check for blacklisted terms.
  • Interface constraints — Respect character limits, spacing and the practical needs of operational screens.
  • Comparative QA — Check source–target alignment and confirm that translated content is complete.

Key takeaway

In operational workflows, linguistic quality is inseparable from usability. MTPE requires more than correcting wording: the translated instructions must follow terminology requirements, fit interface constraints and remain clear enough for agents to use in context.
