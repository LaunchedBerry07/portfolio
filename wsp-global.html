Project overview

Clear, consistent professional communication

This assignment involved linguistic revision and proofreading of corporate and professional documents for WSP Global, an organization working across engineering, science and infrastructure.

The review focused on correcting linguistic issues, checking wording in context and applying Canadian French conventions appropriate to professional business content. The aim was to deliver clear, coherent and polished text while preserving the intended meaning.

At a glance

  • Client: WSP Global
  • Language direction: English → Canadian French
  • Documented volume: 4,942 words
  • Services: Linguistic revision and proofreading
  • Content context: Corporate and professional documents

Linguistic focus

Three priorities in the review

  • Accuracy — Identify and correct linguistic issues while preserving the source content’s intended meaning.
  • Contextual coherence — Check that wording remains clear and appropriate within the surrounding professional material.
  • Professional style — Apply Canadian French conventions and maintain a polished register suited to an engineering-related business context.

Key takeaway

Effective proofreading goes beyond correcting isolated errors. It helps preserve meaning, strengthen clarity and maintain a consistent professional voice in the final text.
