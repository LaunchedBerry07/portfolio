Project overview

Consistent terminology across institutional mandates

This body of work comprised 14 institutional and public-sector mandates totalling 101,450 words. The group included a 43,521-word core project. The translation work required attention to official government terminology, established stylistic conventions and an appropriate administrative register across separate deliveries.

TERMIUM Plus, the Government of Canada's terminology and linguistic data bank, was consulted to research and verify specialized terms. This reference-based approach supported terminological consistency and alignment with institutional usage.

At a glance

Linguistic focus

Three priorities for institutional translation

  • Official terminology: Research specialized terms against Government of Canada terminology resources, including TERMIUM Plus.
  • Administrative register: Follow the stylistic conventions and formal register expected in institutional content.
  • Consistency across deliveries: Carry terminology and editorial decisions across related mandates while keeping the wording appropriate to each context.

Key takeaway

Reliable institutional translation depends on traceable terminology research, an appropriate administrative register and consistent decisions across separate deliveries.
