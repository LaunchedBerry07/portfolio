The Challenge

Clarity in a professional context

Linguistic revision involves more than identifying grammatical errors. A text may be technically understandable while still requiring adjustments to its wording, sentence structure, terminology or overall flow.

In professional content associated with engineering and infrastructure services, clarity and consistency are particularly important. Readers need to understand the information being communicated without being distracted by awkward phrasing, inconsistent usage or stylistic choices that do not suit the context.

The challenge in this assignment was to review the French content with attention to three complementary dimensions:

  • Linguistic accuracy: identifying issues in grammar, syntax, vocabulary and usage.
  • Contextual coherence: ensuring that wording conveyed the intended meaning clearly and consistently.
  • Professional style: maintaining a register and tone appropriate for corporate and professional communication.

The objective was to improve the quality and readability of the French text while respecting the meaning of the source content.

Working with Language and Context

Reading beyond the individual sentence

A successful revision must consider how individual sentences contribute to the meaning and coherence of the complete text.

Correcting an isolated grammatical issue is not always sufficient. A sentence may be technically correct but remain difficult to read, imprecise in context or inconsistent with the tone of the surrounding content.

My approach to professional revision focuses on the relationship between linguistic accuracy, intended meaning and readability. The goal is to produce French that reads naturally and communicates clearly without introducing changes that alter the source meaning.
