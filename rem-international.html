Project overview

Terminological consistency across related product families

The documented scope covers 51,187 words: 25,693 words of technical translation and 25,494 words of comparative editing.

The documentation addressed screw conveyors, sludge management, water treatment and polymer preparation. A key challenge was maintaining consistent nomenclature across related equipment families and specialized industrial applications.

Translation and comparative editing served complementary purposes: translating the material into French and checking the translated text against the source to identify discrepancies and protect technical distinctions.

At a glance

  • Client: R.E.M. International Mechanics
  • Language direction: English → French
  • Technical translation: 25,693 words
  • Comparative editing: 25,494 words
  • Combined documented volume: 51,187 words
  • Subject areas: Screw conveyors; sludge management; water treatment; polymer preparation

Linguistic focus

  • Nomenclature: Maintain stable terminology across related equipment families and product codes.
  • Technical context: Preserve distinctions between industrial processes, equipment and applications.
  • Comparative review: Check the target text against the source instead of relying on proofreading alone.

Key takeaway

In industrial documentation, a term may identify a specific component, product family or function. Comparative review helps protect those distinctions and supports consistency across related material.
