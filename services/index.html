The heart of my practice

Four pillars of linguistic quality.

Language work is never just about individual words. It takes context, sound judgment and consistent attention to the details that make content work.

01 Core service

Translation & Localization

Accurate meaning. Natural language. Content that fits its audience.

I translate English-language content into French, specializing in Canadian French while also working with Quebec French, European French and International French. I localize digital, technical, institutional, commercial and editorial material, adapting terminology, language conventions and content to the intended audience and context.

Services include

  • General and specialized translation
  • Technical manuals and documentation
  • Website, software and user interface localization
  • Interactive content and user journeys
  • Canadian French and other French-variety adaptation, as defined by the brief

Language & context

French variety selectionCanadian French is my main specialization. Quebec French, European French and International French are also available according to the brief, audience and intended market.
Context-sensitive localizationTerminology, regional conventions, tone and interface constraints are considered together so localized content feels appropriate in its intended setting.

02 Core service

Editing, Comparative Revision & Proofreading

Clearer writing. Consistent language. Greater confidence in every detail.

Linguistic quality goes beyond spelling and grammar. Depending on the assignment, I compare target content with its source, edit a text within its own language or carry out a final proofread. Each intervention follows the project brief, language variety and applicable editorial conventions.

Comparative revisionChecks the target text against the source for accuracy, completeness and consistency.
Monolingual editingImproves clarity, flow, style and readability without necessarily revisiting the source.
ProofreadingChecks the near-final text for remaining linguistic and typographical issues.

Additional support

  • Linguistic quality assurance (LQA)
  • Terminology and style-guide compliance
  • Linguistic consultation and fact-checking within agreed scope

03 Core service

MTPE & Linguistic Quality Assurance

Machine-generated content, refined through human linguistic judgment.

Machine translation can accelerate content production, but its output must be assessed in context. Terminology, intended meaning, tone and the consequences of an error all matter. Linguistic quality assurance adds structured checks against source content, project instructions and established requirements.

Full post-editingDetailed correction and refinement to the agreed publication-ready quality level.
Light post-editingTargeted intervention focused on comprehension and the agreed use of the content.
Linguistic QAChecks content against source material, terminology and project-specific criteria.

04 Core service

Terminology Management & Editorial Control

Consistent language starts with shared references.

Consistent terminology is essential when content spans multiple documents, products, teams or delivery cycles. I work with terminology resources, translation memories, style guidance and project-specific rules to support consistency across related content.

Areas of support include

  • Glossary and terminology management
  • Terminology research and validation
  • Translation memory resources within language projects
  • Style-guide and editorial-rule compliance
  • Repeatable linguistic quality checks

Keeping language consistent

Terminology research & validationTerms are checked against reliable references, project documentation and the conventions required for the intended audience.
Shared language resourcesGlossaries, translation memories and style guidance help maintain consistency across related content, contributors and delivery cycles.
