Rates & Terms

The rates listed above are in Canadian dollars (CAD). Final pricing depends on volume, content type, resources, context and timeline.

A $45 CAD minimum charge applies to all micro-projects. A 25%–50% surcharge applies to rush deliveries, including weekends, evenings or turnarounds under 24 hours. Translation-memory discounts may apply when a CAT-tool analysis report is provided.

The data annotation rate applies only to clearly defined, rubric-based text or data labelling tasks. Specialist medical, legal, scientific or safety-related annotation and advanced AI model evaluation are excluded.

No fixed rate is published for AI instruction design, workflow automation or technical implementation. These projects are scoped individually according to the project brief.
